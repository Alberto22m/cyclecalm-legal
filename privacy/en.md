# CycleCalm Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 1, 2026**

[English](/cyclecalm-legal/privacy/en/) · [Русский](/cyclecalm-legal/privacy/ru/) · [Español](/cyclecalm-legal/privacy/es/) · [Français](/cyclecalm-legal/privacy/fr/)

## In short

CycleCalm (“the app”) is a private period and cycle tracker. **Your data stays on your device.** The app has no account, no developer servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third‑party trackers. We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal data.

## What the app stores

When you use the app you may enter dates of your periods, symptoms, mood, discharge, temperature, weight, medications, notes and settings. This information is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. The developer cannot see it or access it.

## Optional features

- **iCloud sync (Premium, optional).** If you turn it on, your data is stored in your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database, which is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot access it.
- **Apple Health (optional).** If you grant permission, the app can read and write menstrual data in Apple Health. This data stays between you, the app on your device and Apple Health. The developer does not receive it.
- **Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can hide their content in Settings.
- **Face ID, Touch ID and PIN.** Biometric checks are handled by iOS and the app never receives your biometric data. A PIN, if you set one, is stored in your device’s Keychain.
- **Export and import.** CSV files and PDF reports are created on your device only when you ask for them. You decide where to send or save them (for example with the iOS share sheet). Once a file leaves the app, it is under your control.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple (App Store). We do not receive your name, Apple ID, payment card or other payment details. Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

## What we do not do

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use third‑party SDKs for analytics or advertising.

## Children

CycleCalm is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them. Because the app does not collect data, there is nothing to collect from anyone.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything in the app under **Settings → Delete all data**. Deleting the app also removes its local data. If you used iCloud sync, you can remove the app’s iCloud data in your iPhone’s iCloud settings (**Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage**).

## Security

Data is stored in your device’s protected storage and benefits from iOS device encryption. You can add a PIN, Face ID or Touch ID lock in the app. Please keep your device and Apple ID secure.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your data directly in the app and in your Apple account. If you have a question about this policy, contact us using the link below.

## Medical disclaimer

CycleCalm provides general information and predictions. It is not a medical device and not a method of contraception, and it does not replace advice from a doctor.

## Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the date above. The latest version is always available at this address.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/Alberto22m/cyclecalm-legal/issues>. Please do not post personal or health information in a public issue.
